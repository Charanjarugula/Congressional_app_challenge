Common questions

How do I report or block someone?

Open their profile from a hit, a chat, or People, tap the “…” button, and choose Report or Block. To report a single message, long-press it in the chat and tap Report. Blocking hides you from each other in Explore, People and new chats; a hit you already share stays until you leave it.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open the Settings tab, scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account. Your profile, photos, friendships, and direct messages are removed right away.

I didn’t get my sign-in code

The code is 6 digits and arrives from Courtly within a minute. Check your spam folder, and tap Resend code if it has been more than a minute. Each code works for 10 minutes.

Who can use Courtly?

Players 18 and older.

How do friend requests work?

Tap Add on someone’s profile and it changes to Requested until they accept. Tap Requested to take it back. Once they accept, you can message each other.

How do I stop showing up for players near me?

On the Settings tab, set “Who can see my hits” to Friends only. Your hits then only show to friends, and you stop being suggested to players nearby.

Can I use Courtly without sharing my location?

Yes. Type a city or ZIP code instead. Your profile shows your city. A hit’s chosen court or meeting address is shared with players who can view that hit.

What do you do with my contacts?

Only hashed email addresses are compared to find friends already on Courtly. Your address book is never uploaded or stored.

Where can I play?

When you plan a hit, search for a court, place, or exact street address, then check the pin on the map. You can add the court name, surface, and lighting details. Courtly helps you coordinate a hit; it does not reserve a court or guarantee access.
