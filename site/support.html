Support

Email courtly.support@gmail.com and we will get back to you within a day. Reports of abusive behavior are reviewed within 24 hours.

Common questions

How do I report or block someone?
Open their profile from a hit, a chat, or People, tap the “…” button, and choose Report or Block. Blocking hides you from each other everywhere in the app.

How do I delete my account?
In the app, go to You → Account → Delete account. Your profile, photos, friendships, and direct messages are removed right away.

Can I use Courtly without sharing my location?
Yes. Type a city or ZIP code instead. Other players only ever see your city.

What do you do with my contacts?
Only hashed phone numbers and emails are compared to find friends already on Courtly. Your address book is never uploaded or stored.

Where can I play?
Courtly lists public courts across Palm Beach County. If yours is missing, add it when you plan a hit.
