Privacy Policy

Last updated October 14, 2026

Courtly (“we”, “us”) is a tennis matchmaking app for Palm Beach County, Florida. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We collect only what the app needs to work, and we do not sell or share your data for advertising.

Who can use Courtly

Courtly is for players 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address (or the Apple or Google account you sign in with) and a sign-in token.
  • Profile: first and last name, birthday (to confirm you are 13 or older), gender (optional, “prefer not to say” is available), tennis level, what you like to play, an optional profile photo, and an optional phone number.
  • Area: the ZIP code or city you play in, stored as the center point of that area. We never store your exact location. Other players only see your city.
  • Availability: the weekly blocks you mark as free. If you connect your calendar, the app reads whether you are free or busy during those blocks on your device and shares only a free-or-busy flag per block for the coming week, which other players see as part of your availability. Event titles and details never leave your phone.
  • Contacts (optional): to find friends who already use Courtly, the app hashes the phone numbers and emails in your address book on your phone and sends only those hashes to our servers to compare. Your contacts’ hashes are compared and discarded, and your address book is never uploaded. Your own hashed sign-in email, and the hashed phone number if you add one under Account, stay on your profile so friends can find you; the email hash has no opt-out, and you can remove your number at any time.
  • Hits and chats: the hits you post or join, your messages, and who you add as a friend, block, or report.
  • Device: a push notification token if you turn notifications on.

How we use it

  • To show you hits near you and let other players find yours.
  • To connect you with people you choose to play with, and to run the chat for confirmed hits.
  • To send the notifications you turn on: requests, acceptances, reminders, cancellations, messages, and the day-after check-in.
  • To keep the community safe: handling reports and blocks, and limiting abuse.

What other players see

Your first name and last initial, your photo if you added one, your level, your city, how many hits you have played, what you like to play, and your usual availability. Your birthday, email, phone number, exact location, and calendar details are never shown to other players.

Where it lives

Your data is stored with Supabase in the United States. Sign-in is handled by Apple, Google, or a one-time code emailed to you. Push notifications go through Apple. We do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

Your choices

  • Permissions: location, contacts, calendar, and notifications are all optional. Courtly works without them; you can type a ZIP code instead of sharing location.
  • Block and report: from any player’s profile. Blocking hides you from each other in Play, People and new chats, and neither of you is told. A hit you already share, and its chat, stays as it is until you leave it.
  • Delete your account: in the app under You → Account → Delete account. Deletion removes your profile, photos, friendships, and direct messages right away. Messages you sent in group or hit chats stay visible to the other players without your name. Reports made about an account are kept for 90 days for safety, then deleted; the no-show count on a profile stays.
  • Questions: email courtly.support@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
