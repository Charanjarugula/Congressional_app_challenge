Privacy Policy

Last updated October 16, 2026

Courtly (“we”, “us”) is an app for finding tennis partners, built in Palm Beach County, Florida, and available in the United States. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We collect only what the app needs to work, and we do not sell or share your data for advertising.

Who can use Courtly

Courtly is for players 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address (or the Apple or Google account you sign in with) and a sign-in token.
  • Profile: first and last name, birthday (only to confirm you are 18 or older; where the law requires it, Apple also tells us whether you are 18 or older, and nothing else about your age), tennis level, what you like to play, and an optional profile photo. Courtly does not ask for your phone number.
  • Area: the ZIP code or city you play in, stored as the center point of that area. If that center can’t be looked up, we keep your location rounded to roughly a kilometer instead, and never anything more precise. Other players only see your city.
  • Availability: the weekly blocks you mark as free, and your iPhone’s time zone so those blocks match your local time. If you connect your calendar, the app reads whether you are free or busy during those blocks on your device and shares only a free-or-busy flag per block for the coming week, which other players see as part of your availability. Event titles and details never leave your phone.
  • Contacts (optional): to find friends who already use Courtly, the app hashes the email addresses in your address book on your phone and sends only those hashes to our servers to compare with players’ hashed sign-in emails. The hashes are compared and discarded, and your address book is never uploaded. Phone numbers are not used for matching.
  • Hits and chats: the hits you post or join, the court or meeting location you choose (including its address and map coordinates), your messages, and who you add as a friend, block, or report. If you report a message, its text is kept with the report.
  • Support: information you send when asking for help or reporting a problem.
  • Device: a push notification token if you turn notifications on.

How we use it

  • To show you hits near you and let other players find yours.
  • To connect you with people you choose to play with, and to run the chat for confirmed hits.
  • To send the notifications you turn on: requests, acceptances, reminders, cancellations, messages, and the day-after check-in.
  • To keep the community safe: handling reports and blocks, and limiting abuse.

What other players see

Your first name and last initial, your photo if you added one, your level, your city, how many hits you have played, and what you like to play. Only your friends see your usual availability. Your birthday, sign-in email, device location, and calendar details are not shown to other players. A hit’s chosen court or meeting address and map location are visible to players who can view that hit. Choose a location you are comfortable sharing.

Other players can find you by searching your name. If “Who can see my hits” is set to Anyone, players within about 25 miles may also see you suggested as someone who plays near them. Set it to Friends only on the Settings tab to stop that.

Where it lives

Your data is stored with Supabase in the United States. Sign-in is handled by Apple, Google, or a one-time code emailed to you. Sign-in codes and safety reports (including a reported message’s text) are sent by email through Google Gmail. Push notifications go through Apple. We do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

Your choices

  • Permissions: location, contacts, calendar, and notifications are all optional. Courtly works without them; you can type a city or ZIP code instead of sharing location.
  • Block and report: from any player’s profile (the “…” button), or long-press a message to report it. Blocking hides you from each other in Explore, People and new chats, and neither of you is told. A hit you already share, and its chat, stays as it is until you leave it.
  • Delete your account: in the app: open the Settings tab, scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account. Deletion removes your profile, photos, friendships, and direct messages right away. Messages you sent in group or hit chats stay visible to the other players without your name. Reports made about an account are kept for 90 days for safety, then deleted; the no-show count on a profile stays.
  • Questions: email courtly.support@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
